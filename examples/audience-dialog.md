# Audience and workplace interaction example

The choices below are prompts, not defaults. Free-form input always takes priority.

## 1. Target role

**Question:** Who is this exam primarily for? This controls terminology depth, workplace situations, distractors, and expected decisions.

Example choices derived from the material:

- Operations engineer
- Implementation engineer
- Technical support engineer
- Solution architect

## 2. Experience level

**Question:** How much relevant experience do the examinees have?

- Completed training with little practice
- Basic knowledge and limited practice
- Independently handles common tasks
- Handles complex diagnosis or design

## 3. Primary workplace scenario

**Question:** Where should examinees apply this knowledge most often?

- Daily operations
- Deployment and delivery
- Pre-production validation
- Customer issue handling

## 4. Exact paper structure

**Question:** Confirm the proposed allocation: 8 single-choice, 6 multiple-choice, 2 true/false, and 4 short-answer/case questions; 20 questions total.

Generation begins only after the audience context and exact allocation are confirmed. Before substantial work, explain that option design and multi-stage quality review may take time and provide stage-based progress updates.
